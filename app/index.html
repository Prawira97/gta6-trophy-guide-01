    Phase 1 — Story & side missions 60–80 h

    Play the story with Jason and Lucia. Story missions need no gold medals, so enjoy the campaign — but take the optional missions as they appear, scan new vehicles with the WAiNK app, and study new animals through your binoculars from the start: 100% completion later needs 200 of the 277 base vehicle models and 100 of the 255 animals, and everything you do before the ending already counts.

    Unlocks on the way: the 12 story trophies, plus Master of None, Whip It Good and the first side-character questlines.

    Phase 2 — 100% completion ~100 h

    After the ending, Progress > Stats gains a 100% Completion entry. You do not have to find everything — the checklist asks for selected portions only (see the table below). Every robbery, underwater discovery, shooting range, drone race and Bret quest is required; only 70 of the 80 Challenges are needed, so skip the nastiest ten.

    Unlocks on the way: A Long Road (gold) and most collectible and activity trophies.

    Phase 3 — Strangers & cleanup 10–20 h

    Finish the final missions for Andy, Kenton, Billy, Randy and Tyrone, complete eight qualifying Strangers & Freaks strands, then mop up whatever the checklist did not cover. Check the list above for anything left, and the platinum — Vice Got a Price! — pops.

    Unlocks on the way: the five side-character finales and Strangers & Freaks (silver).

    ⚠ The one trophy you can miss: Positive Mental Attitude. After the early Chapter 1 mission Down to Earth, Lucia mentions meditation in the kitchen at Jason’s house one morning and heads out to the balcony. Follow her, accept the prompt, and meditate there before leaving Chapter 1 — it cannot be done again afterwards, and it is not yet confirmed whether the trophy needs it. Keep a manual save at the start and end of every chapter: there is no mission replay, and cheats disable trophies and saving. Source: KeenGamer; GameMapa / PowerPyx.
